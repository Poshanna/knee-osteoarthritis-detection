### **Slide 4: Abstract**

This research presents a comprehensive deep learning solution for the automatic detection and classification of Knee Osteoarthritis (OA) using X-ray images.

**Key Contributions:**
- Developed a Dual-Stream EfficientNetV2 architecture with Feature Pyramid Network and Dual Attention mechanisms.
- Achieved **92.95% accuracy** on 5-class KL grade classification.
- Implemented **Grad-CAM explainability** to highlight clinical pain areas (osteophytes, joint narrowing).
- Created an **interactive Streamlit application** for clinical diagnosis assistance.
- Validated on a dataset of **8,260 X-ray images** across KL Grades 0-4.

---

### **Slide 5: Background - Osteoarthritis**

**What is Osteoarthritis (OA)?**
- Osteoarthritis is the **most common form of arthritis**, affecting millions worldwide.
- Occurs when the **protective cartilage** that cushions bone ends wears down over time.
- Most commonly affects joints in **hands, knees, hips, and spine**.

**Impact on Patients:**
- Causes pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility.
- Progressive disease - early detection is critical for management.
- **Knee OA** is a leading cause of disability in elderly populations.

---

### **Slide 6: Background - KL Grading System**

The **Kellgren-Lawrence (KL) Grading System** is the gold standard for classifying knee OA severity:

| Grade | Severity | Radiographic Features |
|-------|----------|----------------------|
| **0** | Normal | No features of OA |
| **1** | Doubtful | Possible osteophytic lipping, doubtful JSN |
| **2** | Minimal | Definite osteophytes, possible JSN |
| **3** | Moderate | Multiple osteophytes, definite JSN, sclerosis |
| **4** | Severe | Large osteophytes, marked JSN, severe sclerosis |

*JSN = Joint Space Narrowing*

### **Slide 11: Workflow & Model Architecture**

**System Workflow:**

```
[X-ray Image] → [Preprocessing] → [CLAHE Enhancement] → [Model Input]
                                                            ↓
[Clinical Report] ← [Grad-CAM] ← [Prediction] ← [Dual-Stream CNN]
                                        ↓
                              [KL Grade: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4]
```

**Proposed Model Architecture:**
1. **Backbone:** EfficientNetV2-S (pretrained on ImageNet)
2. **Feature Pyramid Network (FPN):** Multi-scale feature extraction
3. **Dual Attention:**
   - Channel Attention: Important feature channels
   - Spatial Attention: Critical image regions
4. **Structural Edge Stream:** Canny Edge Detection CNN
5. **Fusion:** Concatenation + Fully Connected Classifier
